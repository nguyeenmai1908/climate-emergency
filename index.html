Module Overview

Climate education begins with relationship

This week asks a foundational question: what does the climate emergency actually mean for an educator? Not just what to teach — but how to be present to it.

Complete this module before your first class. You'll read Ailton Krenak's Ideas to Postpone the End of the World, watch a 45-minute video on Earth as a superorganism, and sit with one reflection question that will seed your first in-class conversation.

Key Ideas

Three threads for this week

3 of 3 ideas
Idea 01
Earth as Organism, Not Resource
Krenak argues we have severed ourselves from Earth by treating it as property or raw material. His people's river, Watu, is not a resource — it is a grandfather. This relational ontology is the starting point for the whole course.
Idea 02
The "Humanity Club" & Its Exclusions
The idea of a singular, universal "humanity" has historically been used to justify colonization, displacement, and the erasure of Indigenous ways of knowing. Who gets counted as fully human — and who doesn't?
Idea 03
Dreaming as Resistance
For Krenak, holding onto story, song, and dream is not escapism — it is how Indigenous peoples have survived five hundred years of colonization. It is also how we "postpone the end of the world."
