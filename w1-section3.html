The Superorganism & the Future
45 minutes

You've read Krenak. Now watch this.

This video offers a complementary lens — scientific and systems-oriented — on the same questions Krenak raises through story and philosophy. Where Krenak speaks from within a living relationship with the Earth, this video steps back to examine Earth as an integrated living system. Let the two perspectives sit alongside each other as you watch.

They do not need to agree. The productive friction between them is part of what we'll explore in class.

Watch with these questions in mind
Three things to pay attention to
  1. How does this video's framing of Earth and life connect with or diverge from Krenak's? What gets added, and what gets left out?
  2. What does thinking about Earth as a "superorganism" make possible — and what might it close off? Who speaks in this framing, and who doesn't?
  3. What would it mean to teach from this perspective in a K–12 classroom? How might you pair it with what you've just read?
